I am a Structural Engineer with 5 years of professional experience, including 4 years of US project experience, specializing in structural analysis, design, engineering calculations, and technical review.

My professional experience includes working on structural systems involving reinforced concrete, structural steel, seismic design, wind analysis, and structural modelling. I have been involved in engineering design, calculation verification, model review, and preparation of technical documentation for projects following US and international design standards.

Beyond conventional structural engineering, I am interested in combining engineering principles with programming, computational methods, and interactive visualization to develop tools that make structural analysis more efficient, transparent, and accessible.

My Goal

My goal is to bridge the gap between traditional structural engineering and computational engineering by building practical, transparent, and technically verifiable engineering tools.

I believe the future of structural engineering lies not only in understanding how structures behave but also in developing better ways to analyse, visualize, automate, and communicate their behaviour.

All tools and demonstrations published here are intended for educational, research, and preliminary engineering applications. Independent engineering verification is necessary before practical use.
