# Naveen Suthar

**Structural Engineer | Structural Analysis & Design | Computational Engineering**

Structural Engineer with **5 years of professional experience, including 4 years of US project experience**, specializing in structural analysis, design, engineering calculations, and technical review.

I hold an **M.Tech in Structural Engineering from Delhi Technological University (DTU)** and a **B.Tech in Civil Engineering from Rajasthan Technical University (RTU)**.

My professional experience covers reinforced concrete and structural steel structures, seismic and wind analysis, structural modelling, calculation verification, and technical documentation in accordance with US and international design standards.

I am passionate about integrating structural engineering with programming, computational methods, and interactive visualization to develop practical tools that improve engineering workflows and make structural behaviour easier to understand and validate.
